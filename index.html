Overview

EarthAlign is a large-scale Earth system-language alignment dataset and benchmark for learning and evaluating multimodal representations of Earth system data. It contains 553,738 samples spanning 2000-2024, with broad geographic coverage and diverse natural phenomena and normal weather conditions. Each sample includes temporal and geospatial information, multivariate ERA5 data, and four metadata-grounded caption styles that describe the same Earth system condition from different perspectives. Building on EarthAlign, MeteoLIP is a dual-encoder framework for aligning meteorological data with natural language. Its meteorology tokenizer, MetTok, aggregates variable-specific representations into compact meteorology tokens while incorporating temporal and geospatial information. MeteoLIP supports cross-modal retrieval, weather-condition classification, and physical variable estimation, providing a benchmark for evaluating language-aligned Earth system representations.

🏆 Highlights

  1. EarthAlign Dataset. 553,738 samples spanning 2000-2024, with broad geographic coverage and diverse natural phenomena and normal weather conditions. Four metadata-grounded caption styles provide complementary descriptions of each Earth system condition.
  2. MeteoLIP. A CLIP/SigLIP-based dual-encoder framework for meteorology-language representation learning, with MetTok for meteorological tokenization and temporal/geospatial conditioning.
  3. Three Evaluation Tasks. Cross-modal retrieval, weather-condition classification, and physical variable estimation.
  4. Open Release. Dataset, training and evaluation code, and pretrained model weights are publicly available.

EarthAlign Data Construction

EarthAlign combines expert-curated natural-event records with climatology-based normal-weather samples. The resulting records contain standardized spatial and temporal information, labels, four grounded caption styles for alignment with Earth system data. Each record is paired with 63-variable ERA5 snapshots.

MeteoLIP Architecture and Training

MeteoLIP consists of transformer-based meteorological and text encoders. MetTok converts multivariate meteorological input into a compact token sequence. During training, we randomly sample a caption style for each meteorological input and optimize a contrastive objective to align meteorological and textual representations in a shared embedding space.
